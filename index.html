Data & decisions

Ask your data in plain English

Anyone on staff asks a sales or operations question in plain English and gets a table or chart back, without waiting on IT.

36 of 36 test questions correct before release, and every real question audited after launch.

How it works

A model turns the question into a database query, a safety filter checks it, and it runs through a view-only account.

Guardrail

Company sign-in plus an allowlist. Every answer has a "check how I got this" panel. A test set must pass before every release.

Customer support

A helpdesk that answers, then learns

Answers incoming support tickets from the help center, and turns staff corrections into better articles every week.

Hundreds of tickets a month get an instant answer, and 24% are fully solved by AI.

How it works

A new ticket triggers a workflow that finds the most relevant help articles, reads any attached photos, drafts the reply, and rates its own confidence. Each week, tickets staff marked as wrong or worth keeping become help-article fixes.

Guardrail

Low confidence only acknowledges and hands off to a person. Anything that needs approval, and any billing dispute, is never promised.

What I learned

A blind test on tickets the AI had never seen caught problems no demo would have. Now no help-article change ships without one.

Medium effortTypically 4 to 6 weeks for a new company

Data & decisions

A second AI that checks every number

Makes sure every number that leaves the team is right before it reaches leadership or partners.

Caught wrong location IDs and records marked inactive by mistake before they reached leadership. Fees matched to the penny.

How it works

One AI builds the numbers. A separate, read-only AI writes its own queries and reports pass or fail. IDs are checked against names, and totals are tied to a second source.

Guardrail

Standing rule: no report, payout, or fee file goes out without an independent check.

What I learned

An earlier daily AI insights feed raised false alarms and had to be paused. AI conclusions need a checker before anyone sees them, so now every number gets one.

Low effortTypically 1 to 3 weeks for a new company

How I work

One team, one win, then the next

The method I'm using to take AI company-wide: small pilots, real tests, and a champion on every team.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Staff tell us what busywork they want off their plate, through a short form. The best ideas come from the people doing the work.

  2. 2

    Score

    Every idea is scored on four tests: it happens often, it's text in and text out, it follows rules, and a mistake is cheap to catch.

  3. 3

    Pilot

    One team first, with a named champion. Start with AI you already pay for, and build custom only where it pays off.

  4. 4

    Prove, then scale

    Blind tests and an independent checker before anything goes live. Then hand it to the team and move to the next one.

House rules, on every build

  • AI reads your data, it doesn't change it. Your database stays read-only for AI. It only writes to its own separate tables.
  • A person approves what goes out. Nothing reaches a customer, vendor, or partner without a human yes.
  • Every number gets a second checker. One AI builds the numbers. A separate one checks them against a second source.
  • Low confidence goes to a human. When the AI isn't sure, it says so and hands off instead of guessing.
