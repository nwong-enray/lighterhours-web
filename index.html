IT & security

One screen showing what every AI helper is doing

When several AI agents work at once, the hard part is knowing which one is waiting on a person. This puts every agent on one screen.

In its first two days, 36 replies went to waiting agents straight from the one screen.

How it works

Every AI helper shows up on one screen, with what it's working on and whether it needs a person. You can answer it right there, without hunting through windows.

Guardrail

Agents stop and wait for a person's answer. Nothing counts as done until someone marks it handled.

What we learned

The slow part of running several AI agents wasn't the AI. It was agents sitting idle, waiting on an answer nobody noticed. Making that wait visible is what kept the work moving.

Medium effortTypically 1 to 2 weeks for a new company

Data & decisions

Price changes tested before a single customer sees them

A pricing simulator that shows, location by location, what a menu price change would earn, using real sales, what every item costs to make, and nearby competitors' prices.

Owners try a price plan and see the total gain, the safe ceiling for each location, and which ones shouldn't raise at all, in minutes instead of a multi-week study.

How it works

It models each item's sales, price, and cost at every location, then replays a price plan at four walk-away rates, from nobody leaving to a worst case. Nearby shops' prices are checked for each location.

Guardrail

It shows a range, never one magic number, and checks past price changes against what it predicted. Profit is clearly marked as before fees and labor, so nobody mistakes it for take-home.

What we learned

The answer that mattered most was which locations should hold. A raise that pays at one location can cost customers at another, where nearby competitors are already cheaper.

Medium effortTypically 3 to 5 weeks for a new company

Operations

Emails that chase their own answers

When an email needs someone else's input, the AI works out who has the answer, asks them directly, and picks the work back up the moment they reply.

Running on every piece of work: an answer restarts the job within minutes, with no one playing middleman.

How it works

The AI works out who has the missing answer, asks them, and picks the work back up the moment they reply. The reply is written only from facts it has checked.

Guardrail

Every fact in the reply is checked against the systems it came from. If something can't be verified, it asks instead of guessing.

What we learned

The slow part of most emails isn't writing the reply. It's waiting on one person's answer and remembering to check. Now the answer itself restarts the work.

Medium effortTypically 2 to 3 weeks for a new company

Customer support

A helpdesk that answers, then learns

Answers incoming support tickets from the help center, and turns staff corrections into better articles every week.

Hundreds of tickets a month get an instant answer, and 24% are fully solved by AI. A newer customer-facing version passed 29 of 29 test tickets with zero safety failures. Before it gets near a customer's account, it has to make the same call as an experienced rep: in testing it matched on 47 of 47 past tickets.

How it works

It answers common tickets from the company's own approved help content, and the team's corrections make it better every week.

Guardrail

When it isn't sure, it hands off to a person instead of guessing. Money questions and promises always go to a person.

What we learned

A blind test on tickets the AI had never seen caught problems no demo would have. Now no help-article change ships without one.

Data & decisions

A second AI that checks every number

Makes sure every number that leaves the team is right before it reaches leadership or partners.

Caught wrong location IDs and records marked inactive by mistake before they reached leadership. Fees matched to the penny.

How it works

Whatever builds the numbers never approves them. An independent check confirms every figure before it reaches anyone.

Guardrail

Standing rule: no report, payout, or fee file goes out without an independent check.

What we learned

An earlier daily AI insights feed raised false alarms and had to be paused. AI conclusions need a checker before anyone sees them, so now every number gets one.

How we work

One team, one win, then the next

The method we're using to take AI company-wide: small pilots, real tests, and a champion on every team.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Staff tell us what busywork they want off their plate, through a short form. The best ideas come from the people doing the work.

  2. 2

    Score

    Every idea is scored on four tests: it happens often, it's text in and text out, it follows rules, and a mistake is cheap to catch.

  3. 3

    Pilot

    One team first, with a named champion. Start with AI you already pay for, and build custom only where it pays off.

  4. 4

    Prove, then scale

    Blind tests and an independent checker before anything goes live. Then hand it to the team and move to the next one.

House rules, on every build

  • AI reads your data, it doesn't change it. Your database stays read-only for AI. It only writes to its own separate tables.
  • A person approves what goes out. Nothing reaches a customer, vendor, or partner without a human yes.
  • Every number gets a second checker. One AI builds the numbers. A separate one checks them against a second source.
  • Low confidence goes to a human. When the AI isn't sure, it says so and hands off instead of guessing.
