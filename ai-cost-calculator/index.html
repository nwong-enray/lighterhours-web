What AI automation really costs

How much does AI automation cost a small business?
For most small businesses, the AI itself is the cheapest part. Most jobs cost a few cents to a few dollars each in AI fees, so one workflow usually runs a few dollars to a few tens of dollars a month. The typical small business that pays for AI tools spent about $30 a month on them in 2025. What costs real money is the setup, the rules, and someone keeping it healthy.
What makes an AI automation project cost more?
It comes down to four things. How many systems it touches, how messy your data is, how costly a mistake is, and whether anyone watches it after launch. One inbox is simple. An inbox, a spreadsheet, and your accounting system is a real project.
Is AI automation worth it for a small business?
Run the math on your own week. If a task eats 5 hours of your week and your time is worth $50 an hour, that's about $1,000 a month. If it costs a few tens of dollars a month to run, the only question is how fast the setup pays back. A task that only comes up twice a year may never pay back the setup.
